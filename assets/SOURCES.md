# Asset sources

- The project GitHub icon comes from Bootstrap Icons, <https://github.com/twbs/icons/blob/main/icons/github.svg>, under the MIT license retained in `bootstrap-icons-LICENSE.txt`.

- `bmo.svg`: BMO's official logo from <https://www.bmo.com/dam_asset/asset/9388c217-ca0f-4fbc-90bf-12547e7930e7/bmo-blue-on-transparent-en.svg>. Its SVG viewport isolates the circular symbol; logo geometry and colors are unchanged.
- `arctic-wolf.png`: the reverse Arctic Wolf mark used on its official website, <https://arcticwolf.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/AW_MARK_RV-LOGO_120.png>.
- `cache.png`: Cache's app icon, referenced by its official website, <https://www.cacheinyourcloset.com/assets/app_icon-BTQ11usr.png>.
- `leetcode.png`: official LeetCode reverse mark from <https://leetcode.com/static/images/LeetCode_logo_rvs.png>. The account URL was supplied by Haris; no profile statistics are displayed.
- `uoft-scarborough.svg`: official University of Toronto Scarborough reverse logo used in the university's site header and footer, <https://www.utsc.utoronto.ca/_includes/images/UTSC_rev_optimized.svg>.
- `uoft-crest.svg`: the crest isolated through an SVG viewport from `uoft-scarborough.svg`; its artwork and colors are unchanged. The surrounding plaque and AP Capstone certificate are illustrative HTML/CSS objects rather than original award documents.

- `capybaras.png`: the actual 2055A Capybaras logo from <https://capybaras-2055.vercel.app/logo.png>, linked from the 2055A programming repository.
- `real-madrid.svg`: legacy monochrome favicon from <https://www.realmadrid.com/favicon.svg>; no longer used in the football widget.
- `real-madrid-full-color.png`: full-color Real Madrid crest from ESPN's team-logo service, <https://a.espncdn.com/i/teamlogos/soccer/500/86.png>.
- `erin-mills.png`: Erin Mills Soccer Club's logo from the header of its official website, <https://www.erinmillssoccer.com/>.
- `manchester-united.svg`: official Premier League club badge from <https://resources.premierleague.com/premierleague/badges/t1.svg>.

Club and team marks retain their respective owners' identities. They identify personal interests, not sponsorship or affiliation.

LetsCook's preview embeds its actual deployed homepage: <https://letscook-silk.vercel.app/>. The order book and other project interface illustrations are labeled illustrative; they are not claims of live data or captured screenshots.

VEX awards: <https://mi3lschool.org/history2055A>, filtered to the confirmed 2023–2024 participation years. EmotionSense.AI's award and technology descriptions come from its repository README. RuneVault's description is supported by its README and source; Not a Smartboard's description and contributors come from its README.

- `mario-kart-8.png`: official Nintendo Mario Kart 8 Deluxe key art from the product page's asset URL: <https://www.nintendo.com/us/store/products/mario-kart-8-deluxe-switch/>.
- `mario-standard-kart.png`: Mario's Standard Kart M model render hosted by Super Mario Wiki, <https://mario.wiki.gallery/images/d/db/Standard_Kart_M_%28Mario%29_Model.png>, documented at <https://www.mariowiki.com/Standard_Kart>. The original M badge is preserved.
- `red-bull-racing.svg`: official Oracle Red Bull Racing logo from <https://www.redbullracing.com/_next/static/media/ORBR_logo_2026.4059dac5.svg>.
- `red-bull-car.webp`: transparent side-view car artwork from the official Formula 1 team page, <https://www.formula1.com/en/teams/red-bull-racing>, using its image service at <https://media.formula1.com/image/upload/c_fit,w_800/q_auto/v1740000001/common/f1/2026/redbullracing/2026redbullracingcarright.webp>.
- `haris-khawja-resume-swe.pdf` and `haris-khawja-resume-quant.pdf`: unchanged copies of the two résumés supplied by Haris, added for the requested viewer links.

EmotionSense.AI now embeds <https://emotion-sense-ai.vercel.app/>. RuneVault's dungeon map is illustrative rather than a captured game session. Not a Smartboard's preview is an animated CSS 3D hardware model of a projector and whiteboard, with a Raspberry Pi connected to a laptop on a table. It illustrates the documented setup rather than claiming to be a project photo. A link to the recorded demo remains available in its project details.

The grayscale elixir meter is a decorative game-inspired simulation, independent of the Clash Royale player API. Resource reference: <https://support.supercell.com/clash-royale/en/articles/elixir.html>. Zetamac's changing questions are generated arithmetic; the personal best remains the user-supplied 81. The ten cubic 3D trophies represent the individual awards in `vex.awards`, counting each award separately when an event lists more than one.

The Capybaras trophy cabinet, floor, and cubic four-wheel arm robot are code-native geometry. The cabinet and robot share one CSS 3D camera, with the actual team logo placed on the cabinet glass. The robot illustrates a standard VEX-style mechanism.

The science award's exact title, issuer, research group, and September 2023 date come from Haris's public LinkedIn honors section, <https://ca.linkedin.com/in/hariskhawja>. It lists the University of Toronto Scarborough and the Andre Simpson Research Group; no research topic, publication, or award amount is inferred. Mario Kart and Red Bull Racing are currently commented out in the interest list; their assets remain available for restoration.

Haris supplied the AP Capstone Diploma and AP Research paper topic, ethical AI, on November 12, 2026. Direct credential and paper links are pending. Research is the grouping label; the university recognition retains its official Science Award title inside the widget.
